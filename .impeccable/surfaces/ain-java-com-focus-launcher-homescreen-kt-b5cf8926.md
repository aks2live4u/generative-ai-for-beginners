# Focus Launcher: home, pause, wall, settings

Scope: all Focus Launcher screens (Android, Jetpack Compose). Visitor mode: Operate.
Job: open an essential app in one tap; meet distracting apps with firm-but-kind friction.
Constraints: the user's own font, clock style, wallpaper, note and world clocks are theirs and stay.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A home screen you look up at like a night sky, not a feed you look down into. Refuses the
dark-dashboard default of cards, chips and glowing accents.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-indigo night ground; chalk-white star text; dimmer magnitudes carry hierarchy (size and
brightness, not boxes). Moonlight silver-blue is the calm accent; night-vision ember red-amber marks cost,
limits and late hours. Thin coordinate hairlines separate zones. Manrope for chrome, the user's fonts for
clock, date, note and app names.

STORY: The user sees what matters, opens it, and leaves. When they reach for a distracting app they see
today's cost as stars, breathe while a moon fills, say why, and choose with clear eyes. Late at night
everything gets slower and dimmer.

clocks on a faint plate, a hairline, then pinned apps low in thumb reach, footer with Everything else and
settings. Pause: app name, a row of stars (opens today against the limit), the moon phase filling with the
countdown, then the reason, with going home the filled primary action.
