💧 Cold water reset

Splash cold water on your face, or hold a cold object (ice, a chilled can) against your cheeks and the back of your neck for 20–30 seconds.

This triggers the mammalian dive reflex and can slow a racing heart within seconds.

🖐️ 5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Name them in your head or out loud. Checking them off is optional.

🦶 Feet pressure exercise

Plant both feet flat on the floor. Press down hard through your heels and toes, like you're pushing the floor away. Hold for 10 seconds. Release. Repeat 5 times.

This pulls attention into the body and out of the spiral of thoughts.
