# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

One person (the owner, in India) who uses an Android phone with cloned apps (two WhatsApps) and many apps
they consider important (20+ pinned). They want to spend less time on the phone: fewer mindless unlocks,
less impulse shopping (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra), less aimless browsing and late-night scrolling. All of
these moments matter equally.

## Product Purpose

Focus Launcher replaces the Android home screen. It shows only the apps the user chose, hides everything
else behind a search-only "wall", and puts a short, firm-but-kind pause in front of distracting apps.
Success means less daily screen time and fewer distracting opens, while the phone stays fully usable for
real tasks.

## Positioning

Friction at the moment of temptation, tuned to the kind of app: a browser asks what you are looking up, a
shopping app asks what you need to buy, a chat app asks who you are messaging, and the answer comes back in
the reminder when the chosen time is up. It is a home screen, not a blocker app bolted on.

## Operating Context

Glanced at dozens of times a day, one-handed, often in dim rooms and at night. Sideloaded APK built by
GitHub Actions and installed from a release link. Not on the Play Store.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home screen: clock (5 styles), date, a note, screen time and skip counts, up to 4 world clocks, pinned
  apps as text (1 or 2 columns, up to 30), custom wallpaper (photo, gradient, system), 26 bundled fonts plus
  the phone's Cursive and Casual and an imported font.
- The wall: search-only list of every other app, a short wait before it opens, long-press options.
- Pause: breathing countdown, reasons per app type, intention text for browse/shop/video, session reminder
  (5/10/20 min), daily open limit, strict mode with a 60 s unlock.
- Clone and work-profile apps are listed and launched through LauncherApps.
- Undecided: catching opens from notifications/Recents (needs Accessibility), notification batching,
  grayscale, backup/restore.

## Brand Commitments

- Tone: firm but kind. Real friction, warm words, never guilt-tripping or gamified.
- The user's own font, clock style, wallpaper and note are theirs; the launcher must keep honouring them.
- No feeds, widgets, badges or streak mechanics.

## Evidence on Hand

Screenshots of the user's real home screen (Satisfy-style script font, black wallpaper, two world clocks,
note "Trust the Process", 20+ apps in two columns). No usage studies or testimonials exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Friction belongs at the moment of temptation, not everywhere.
2. Every nudge names the real cost honestly and leaves the decision with the user.
3. The essentials must always be one tap away; never slow down a real task.
4. The home screen is calm by default; nothing on it should compete for attention.
5. Personal expression (fonts, clock, note) is part of why the user keeps the launcher.
